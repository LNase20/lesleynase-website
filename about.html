About

Hi, I'm Lesley Nase

I'm the Chief Relationship Officer at 90210 Enterprise and the founder of Moonspinners Enterprise LLC. Through coaching, speaking, events, and transformational retreats, I create spaces where professionals reconnect with their authentic voice and with themselves.
